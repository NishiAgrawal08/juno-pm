## What it demonstrates

A PM can select a date range, scan feedback grouped into themes across Slack, Jira, and support tickets, and verify each theme against its source evidence.

_____

## Debrief

- **What worked:** Date range and source filters make the scope clear; theme cards connect summaries and scores to evidence the PM can check.
- **What broke / felt like a toy:** It’s labeled demo data, and the AI summary excludes uploaded feedback documents. “Act first” selections also disappear when the page reloads.
- **What I'd change next pass:** Show the number of distinct customer accounts behind each theme alongside its feedback count. That helps the PM tell whether a signal is widespread or mostly coming from one account.
